**Description**: A Browse of the queue carries the speaker's `UpdateID`, which changes when the queue's contents change. The service keeps, per coordinator, the last full reading and the `update_id` its first page carried. `GET /api/rooms/{uid}/queue` answers `{"items", "current", "update_id"}`; on each request it first reads one item to learn the `update_id` and total, and reads the whole queue again only when they differ from the kept reading (or there is none, or the speaker gives no `update_id`); the kept total is the speaker's own, so a queue at or past the 500-item play cap is kept too. With `?since=N`, where N is the `update_id` of a kept reading the probe has just confirmed, it answers `{"unchanged": true, "current", "update_id"}` without items; `current` is always worked out afresh from the room's state. A `since` that isn't a whole number is ignored. A fresh reading never answers unchanged, so a `since` from another coordinator, or from before the service or speaker restarted, that equals the new `update_id` still gets the items. The page sends `since` from its last answer, keeps its rows when told unchanged (clearing a note left by a failed reading), and rebuilds the list only when the items or the current track change, or the user acts on the queue (jump, clear, play). Rows before the current one are dimmed (class `played`, still buttons that play that track), and when the current track changes the list scrolls so the current row is at the top, the played rows above it out of view. The speaker's queue is never changed by this.
