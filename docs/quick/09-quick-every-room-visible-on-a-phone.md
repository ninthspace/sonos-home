# Rooms in one sideways-scrolling row, four in view on a phone

**Date**: 2026-11-04  
**Status**: In Progress  
**Source**: the user in chat (2026-11-04), on their phone: "we need to make it mobile friendly. if i have more than three rooms i can't see the fourth one (office)". At 760 px and below the room strips were one sideways-scrolling row of 8.6 rem strips (style.css `@media (max-width: 760px)`, `#room-list { display: flex; overflow-x: auto }`); four strips are about 36 rem, wider than a 375 px phone, and nothing showed that the row scrolls, so the fourth room was off screen. Spec FR11 (the web UI, usable on a phone), epic 01-10 Story 3's fader criteria (travel at least 120 px at 375 px, `touch-action: none`).  
**Effort**: medium  
**Audit**: pass after fixes (2026-11-04), 2 rounds on the rebuild (the first build's single round passed, see Amended): round 1 (1 Warning: the fader's `touch-action: none` covered about half of each narrow strip, so a finger swipe starting on it couldn't scroll the row, now `pan-x` at 1280 px and below, its touch check part of the [user] criterion; Suggestions taken: a revealed room kept clear of the 2.5 rem fade (scroll-snap removed, as it pulled the room back under it), measured with `getBoundingClientRect` rather than `offsetLeft`, and revealed again when rooms come or go) and round 2 (pass)  
**Amended**: the first build wrapped the strips into rows (four to a row at 375 px, more rooms on further rows), audited and passed; asked what 10 rooms would do (three rows of strips, about 900 px, before the folders), the user said "rooms should be horizontally scrollable" (2026-11-04). Rebuilt as one row at every width that scrolls sideways when the rooms don't fit, with four narrow strips in view on a phone and at 761–1280 px, faded edges where rooms lie past them, and the selected room scrolled into view; the criteria below replace the first build's  

**Description**: The room strips are one row at every width. When they don't fit, the row scrolls sideways (the row only, never the page), an edge fades while there are rooms past it (`app.js` `roomEdges` sets `.more-left` / `.more-right` on scroll, resize and each render), and the selected room is scrolled into the row's view when it is chosen or first shown (`revealSelectedRoom`). At 1280 px and below the strips are narrow (at least 4.4 rem, four across a 375 px phone and the 19.5 rem side column, which was 18.5 rem): the display-only level meter is hidden (the fader and its number show the level; the LED shows playing), names are 1 rem, the padding tighter, the focus ring's offset 3 px; the fader keeps at least 120 px of travel, with `touch-action: pan-x` so that a sideways swipe that starts on a fader scrolls the row (an up/down drag still moves the fader). Above 1280 px the strips are as before (at least 9 rem, four across the 40 rem column) in the same scrolling row. This replaces both the wrapping grid at wider widths and the sideways row of 8.6 rem strips that epic 01-10 Story 3's criterion describes; that story stays as history.
