# A page track keeps its artist when its file has no artist tag

**Date**: 2026-11-04  
**Status**: In Progress

**Description**: `remote.track_label(uri, title, artist)` gives the (title, artist) to show for one of our `/remote/` URLs, from what the speaker reported and the URL's file name, which the service builds as `Title by Artist.ext` (or `Title.ext` when the page names no artist). The speaker's artist always wins. With none: if the speaker's title is the file name itself (tags not read), the name's stem is split at its one " by " (no split when it has none or several); a name long enough that `display_name`'s cap may have cut it (over 225 encoded characters) gives no artist, since the cut may have gone into the artist; if the speaker read a title from the tags and the stem is that title followed by " by ", the rest is the artist; otherwise there is no artist. Anything that isn't one of our `/remote/` URLs is left as the speaker reported it. The queue route and the room state (now playing) both use it, so every page track shows "Title" with "Artist" the same way whether or not the speaker has read its tags.
